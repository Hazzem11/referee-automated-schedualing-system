## Legacy navigation (from “menu box”)

- **PUBLIC**: Executive & Team, Locations
- **MEMBERS / MAIN**: Updates, Rules & Mechanics, Policies & Procedures, Members, Referee Coach List
- **GAMES**: My Games (to referee or evaluate), Availability, Tournaments, Training, Today’s Games
- **EVALUATIONS**: My Games (to evaluate), Evaluation Docs
- **REPORTS**: Lateness, Absence, Incident, Executive Minutes, Constitution
- **ADMIN**: Account, Questions & Interpretations, Dues/Fees, Register, Password, Susp., Logout

## Page notes (from screenshots)

### Updates (home landing)

- Primary content is a list of PDF links (updates)

### My assigned games
- Includes filters (e.g. Future Games / All Games, and confirm/unconfirm flows)
- Table columns visible: Location, Date, Assignment, # Games, Member, Confirmation, Potential Partners, Potential Evaluator

### Rules & Mechanics
